Updated as I go. No star ratings — the note is the rating.

## Reading

<div class="list-section" markdown="1">

**Thinking in Systems** — *Donella Meadows* <span class="list-year">2008</span>
: The book I recommend most; changed how I read every codebase and org chart since.

**The Design of Everyday Things** — *Don Norman* <span class="list-year">1988</span>
: Still the clearest explanation of why so much hardware UX fails.

**Seeing Like a State** — *James C. Scott* <span class="list-year">1998</span>
: Made me rethink how AUTOSAR-style standardization trades local knowledge for legibility.

**The Pragmatic Programmer** — *Hunt & Thomas* <span class="list-year">1999</span>
: Reread every couple of years; ages better than most engineering books.

**Chip War** — *Chris Miller* <span class="list-year">2022</span>
: The geopolitics behind every microcontroller on my desk.

</div>

## Watching

<div class="list-section" markdown="1">

**Chernobyl** — *HBO* <span class="list-year">2019</span>
: The best depiction of a control system failing under organizational pressure I've seen.

**Formula 1: Drive to Survive** — *Netflix* <span class="list-year">2024</span>
: Guilty pleasure; the systems-engineering subplot is better than advertised.

**The Bear** — *FX* <span class="list-year">2024</span>
: Kitchens and ECUs have more in common than either industry admits.

**Kiki's Delivery Service** — *Miyazaki* <span class="list-year">1989</span>
: Rewatch whenever a project needs a reset.

**Blade Runner 2049** — *Villeneuve* <span class="list-year">2017</span>
: For the sound design alone.

</div>

## Listening

<div class="list-section" markdown="1">

**Blackstar** — *David Bowie* <span class="list-year">2016</span>
: The album that got me into signal chains, not just songs.

**In Rainbows** — *Radiohead* <span class="list-year">2007</span>
: Still the reference mix I test new headphones against.

**Kind of Blue** — *Miles Davis* <span class="list-year">1959</span>
: On loop during long simulation runs.

**Currents** — *Tame Impala* <span class="list-year">2015</span>
: Proof a bedroom setup can sound like a studio.

**The Dark Side of the Moon** — *Pink Floyd* <span class="list-year">1973</span>
: The record that started the tube preamp build.

</div>
